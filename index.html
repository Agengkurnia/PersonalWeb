RMPM — Raw Material & Packaging Material Selection System

A comprehensive digital system for managing the full lifecycle of Raw Material (RM) and Packaging Material (PM) selection at Kalbe Nutritionals. As Business Analyst, I designed the end-to-end business flow, created BRD & FSD documentation, and built interactive HTML prototypes used as the blueprint for development. The system covers material evaluation, item specification management, and packaging compliance — replacing manual and fragmented processes across R&D, Quality, and Procurement teams.

RM Selection — Sample Management
4-step wizard workflow: Document Registration → Document Sample → Evaluation → Disposition. Captures supplier info, halal category, GMO status, organic status, EG-DEG content, storage condition, and multi-category test parameters (Organoleptic, Nutrition, Microbiological, Heavy Metals, Pesticides, Antibiotics, Mycotoxin). Disposition step allows type change with audit trail.
Item Spec (RM & PM)
Official quality standard document for each material. Role-based editing: PDV manages material description & allergens, QA/QS manages microbiological & contaminant parameters. Supports "Copy From" reference (Oracle or RM Evaluation). Status flow: Draft → Waiting Approval → Approved → Registered in ERP. Integrated with Oracle Inventory as master data.
Packforces SEAL — PM Selection
The most complete BA project. SEAL (Supplier Evaluation & Approval for Labeling) manages packaging material selection per project. Features: dashboard with status cards (Draft/Submitted/Completed), "My Pending Task" view, multi-PIC assignment per department, document upload with OR-upload + AND-approval logic, timeline audit trail per document, and reference to Item Spec or PM Evaluation.
BA Deliverables: Business Requirements Document (BRD), Functional Specification Document (FSD), role & access matrix per department (PDV, QA/QS, Pack Dev, Regulatory Affairs, Business Dev), business flow diagrams, and fully interactive HTML prototypes — used directly as reference by the development team.
Role: Business Analyst & Developer — Conducted stakeholder interviews across R&D, Quality, and Procurement. Defined business flows, wrote BRD & FSD, designed role-based access rules, and built all HTML prototypes from scratch as the development blueprint.

SVNET — Supplier & Supply Chain Web Platform

An enterprise B2B web platform for Kalbe Nutritionals' Supply Chain division, connecting internal procurement teams with external suppliers. SVNET covers the full supplier lifecycle — from onboarding and registration to procurement notifications, online pitching, and supply chain monitoring. Built on ASP.NET MVC with SQL Server, the platform serves hundreds of suppliers and internal users across multiple business units.

SDB — Supplier Database
Supplier self-registration portal with multi-category support (Direct, Indirect, Packaging Material, Principal). Captures full company profile — address, LOB, currency, employee count, turnover, and contact persons. Role-based access distinguishes internal reviewers from external supplier users.
FPP / FKP — Supply Notifications
Procurement notification system for managing PO-based supply commitments. FPP (supplier-initiated) and FKP (internal-initiated) handle shipment scheduling, quantity balancing, delivery date changes, and multi-tier approval with reject/reason tracking. Includes countdown timer and read/unread status per document.
OPI — Online Pitching
Digital pitching platform where internal teams submit project requests (items, specs, budget, deadlines) and invite registered suppliers to respond. Supports multi-item per project, file attachments, supplier selection, LOB assignment, and a full approval workflow from Draft to Submit.
